# 🛒 Amazon India – Sales & Business Performance Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview
This project presents an interactive **Amazon India Sales & Business Performance Dashboard** created using Microsoft Excel. It provides a visual analysis of sales, profit, customer orders, product performance, and business operations.

The main objective is to convert raw sales data into meaningful business insights that can support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and profitability.
- Identify top-performing product categories.
- Track monthly sales and profit trends.
- Understand customer order status and payment preferences.
- Evaluate state-wise business performance.
- Identify top 10 products based on revenue.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Microsoft Excel**
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Data Analysis
- Data Visualization
- Interactive Dashboard Elements

## 📈 Dashboard Highlights

The dashboard includes the following key performance indicators (KPIs):

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Quantity Sold
- Average Order Value
- Profit Margin

### Key Visualizations
1. Monthly Sales & Profit Trend
2. Category-wise Sales & Profit Performance
3. Top 10 Products by Revenue
4. Order Distribution by Status
5. Sales & Profit by Payment Method
6. State-wise Sales & Profit Performance

## 📌 Key Business Insights

The dashboard helps identify:
- Monthly sales and profitability trends.
- High-revenue product categories.
- Best-performing products.
- Customer payment preferences.
- Order fulfillment and delivery performance.
- State-wise sales opportunities.
